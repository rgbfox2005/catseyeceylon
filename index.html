What we handle

Tell us the product, material and quantity. We search for manufacturers rather than trading companies, check their licences and production capacity, and send you a short list with the reasons behind each pick.

We negotiate in the local language and quote you transparently: unit cost, tooling, packaging and payment terms, laid out so you can compare offers line by line.

We arrange samples before you commit, then inspect production and the finished goods against your specification before anything is shipped.
